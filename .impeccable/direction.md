# Visual direction

## Assigned direction

Build candidate 7 from the Impeccable direction roll: a contemporary editorial index for a personal archive. The site should feel like a well-made reference desk: neutral surfaces, exact rules, calm spacing, and a small orange mark that shows where the reader can go next.

## First viewport

The home opens with the name Sérgio Lopes, the literal line “Artigos, palestras, livros e podcasts.”, a short introduction, and a small natural photograph. Navigation to the four collections is immediately visible. There is no marketing hero, metric wall, decorative slogan, or oversized accent field.

## System

- Dark default: near-black graphite, cool gray surfaces, warm white text.
- Light option: neutral white and cool gray, with the same hierarchy.
- Accent: vivid orange used for active states, links, focus, one small portrait rule, and the primary action.
- Typography: clean sans serif with restrained display scale, short line lengths, and strong weight contrast rather than decorative italics.
- Composition: one-column archive lists with a clear date/type rail, thin rules, and generous vertical rhythm. No heavy card grid.
- Photo: small square portrait with a thin orange edge; it should read as a person in the archive, not a poster image.
- Motion: one subtle page reveal and short state transitions; content stays visible by default and respects reduced motion.

## Interaction

The primary interaction is browsing by section and year. Active navigation, focus, and links use orange; rows reveal their title and source relationship without lifts, shadows, or hover theatrics.

## Constraints

Historical article, podcast, talk, mirror, redirect, and demo content remains unchanged. The redesign only changes the interface around it.
