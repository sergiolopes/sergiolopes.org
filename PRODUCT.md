# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with static GitHub Pages deployment.

## Users

People looking for Sérgio Lopes's published work and past appearances, usually arriving from a search result or an old link and needing a clear way to browse the archive.

## Product Purpose

This site is a durable personal archive of Sérgio Lopes's articles, talks, books, podcasts, and external work. Success means a visitor can quickly understand what is here, find a piece by type or year, and reach the preserved historical material without losing its original context.

## Positioning

The site keeps the work together as a personal record, including local historical copies and links to the original publication or capture when available. It is an archive with context rather than a publication funnel.

## Operating Context

Visitors read long-form pages, scan chronological indexes, open preserved presentations and demos, and follow outbound links to original sites, podcasts, social profiles, and recordings. The archive is updated occasionally rather than as a frequent blog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing archive entries, original titles, dates, URLs, redirects, local snapshots, talks, slide decks, and historical demos.
- Keep articles and podcasts as separate sections, with annual indexes for navigation.
- Keep dark mode and provide a neutral light mode; the interface must remain readable on desktop and mobile.
- Historical PHP and live integrations are intentionally out of scope for this static version.
- Do not invent current biography, metrics, testimonials, or publication claims.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Sérgio Lopes and the site is sergiolopes.org.
- The voice is concise, direct, and personal in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Use the existing photograph of Sérgio Lopes from the archive.
- Use vivid orange as a restrained accent for interaction and wayfinding; the base palette must stay neutral, without brown or sepia surfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

- Archive data and preserved HTML in `src/data/archive.json` and `src/data/mirrors.json`.
- Preserved talk materials in `src/data/talks-external.json`, `src/data/talks-external-links.json`, and `public/talks`.
- Existing photograph at `public/images/sergio-lopes.jpg`.
- Existing routes for the home, articles, podcasts, talks, books, about, external participation, archive, and annual indexes.

## Product Principles

- Treat the work as the subject; the interface should make it easy to reach.
- Keep historical context visible without making the archive feel obsolete or cluttered.
- Prefer clear lists and chronology over decorative dashboards or marketing patterns.
- Keep interface copy short, specific, and human.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts for narrow screens.
